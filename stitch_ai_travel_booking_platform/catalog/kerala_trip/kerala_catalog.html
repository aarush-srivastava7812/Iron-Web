SECTION 5: PLACES TO VISIT
FEATURED EXPERIENCES

Tours & Sightseeing

A chronological tour of cultural and natural marvels — from historic colonial spice trading lanes to palm-fringed backwaters and tea estates.

Day 2 · 09:00 AM – 12:30 PM
Fort Kochi Colonial Heritage Walk

Stroll past 14th-century Chinese fishing nets, visit St. Francis Church (Vasco da Gama's original resting place), and explore Jewish Town spice warehouses.

✓ Certified heritage historian guide
✓ Paradesi Synagogue & Dutch Palace entry
✓ Spiced tea tasting in historical warehouse
Day 3 · 10:00 AM – 02:00 PM
Munnar Tea Plantation & Factory Tasting

Walk along hillside tea trails at 1,600m altitude. Witness artisanal leaf plucking, leaf curling, oxidation, and attend a curated tea sommelier tasting.

✓ VIP Tea Museum entry & factory access
✓ Guided estate walk through private tea gardens
✓ Complimentary tin of premium orthodox tea
Day 5 · 12:00 PM – 05:30 PM
Alleppey Houseboat & Village Canoe Trail

Board a traditional wooden kettuvallam for an intimate cruise through canals. Transfer to a wooden village canoe to explore narrow coir-making hamlets.

✓ Private chartered houseboat with 3 crew members
✓ Freshly cooked on-board lunch & snacks
✓ Guided canoe village excursion
Day 6 · 06:00 PM – 08:00 PM
Kathakali Evening & Kalaripayattu Martial Arts

Witness the theatrical transformation of classical Kathakali artists as natural pigment makeup is applied, followed by ancient Kalaripayattu sword demonstrations.

✓ Front-row reserved seats in heritage theatre
✓ Behind-the-scenes greenroom makeup tour
✓ Interactive photo session with lead dancers
Comfort & Accessibility: Houseboats and private cabs are equipped with shaded canopies and life vests. Walking distances during the heritage tour are gentle (approx. 2.5 km with frequent rest stops).

SECTION 8: DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY
CHRONOLOGICAL PROGRAM

Day-by-Day Schedule

A leisurely sequence balancing scenic road drives, restorative backwater moments, and cultural explorations.

DAY 1 · 12 DEC
Arrival in Kochi & Grand Hyatt Check-In
11:15 AM arrival at Cochin International Airport. Meet chauffeur, transfer to Hotel Grand Hyatt Bolgatty. Afternoon relaxation by the waterfront pool, sunset harbor breeze.
DAY 2 · 13 DEC
Fort Kochi Colonial Heritage & Spice Walk
09:00 AM walking tour of Chinese fishing nets, Jewish Synagogue, and spice markets. Grand 24-dish vegetarian Sadya lunch served on fresh plantain leaf.
DAY 3 · 14 DEC
Scenic Drive to Munnar Tea Highlands
Check-out and ascend the Western Ghats past Cheeyappara & Valara waterfalls. Check into Tea Mount Resort. Evening sunset over tea gardens with hot cardamom brew.
DAY 4 · 15 DEC
Tea Plantation Walk & Mattupetty Dam
Guided stroll through tea estates with leaf pickers. Visit Tata Tea Museum and take a quiet boat cruise along Mattupetty Lake surrounded by eucalyptus hills.
DAY 5 · 16 DEC
Descent to Alleppey & Houseboat Cruise
Morning drive from hills to Alleppey backwaters. Board private kettuvallam houseboat at noon. Feast on fresh Karimeen fish curry while drifting past village hamlets.
DAY 6 · 17 DEC
Canoe Canal Excursion & Kathakali Show
Morning narrow canoe ride through coir-spinning villages. Transfer to coastal resort. Evening attendance at a traditional Kathakali and Kalaripayattu performance.
DAY 7 · 18 DEC
Souvenirs & Departure to New Delhi
Morning purchase of Malabar black pepper, hill spices, and banana chips. Chauffeur transfer to Kochi Airport for 04:30 PM departure flight back to New Delhi.
Tropical Comfort Checklist

Pack breathable cotton or linen clothing, comfortable slip-on walking sandals, sunglasses, and a wide-brim hat. Mosquito repellent lotion is recommended for evening canalside strolls.

Authentic Spices & Tea Buying

Always buy whole spices (cardamom pods, tellicherry pepper, whole cinnamon) vacuum-sealed from licensed spice cooperatives to retain volatile essential oils.
