## Brand & Style

This design system expresses modern nature-immersed luxury and high-touch slow travel, balancing the grounded stability of an heritage expedition house with the rigorous utility of an enterprise-grade booking portal. 

The aesthetic is an editorial-modernist synthesis: warm, tactile paper surfaces juxtaposed against deep evergreen forest canopies, energized by high-frequency electric lime and warm sunset terracotta accents. It rejects the cold sterile blue of generic SaaS and the ephemeral gimmickry of heavy skeuomorphism in favor of high typographic contrast, rich photography layers, generous spatial margins, and deliberate human scrapbook accents (such as subtle rotational tilts and tactile perspective shifts).

The emotional resonance is calm confidence, curatorial care, and dynamic momentum—inviting modern travelers to browse with literary curiosity and transact with clarity.

## Colors

The palette organizes into distinct atmospheric and functional tiers:

- **Earthy Evergreen (`#174f40`)**: The primary brand driver, used for core action buttons, active navigation markers, and brand emblems.
- **Deep Forest Canopy (`#183d34`) & Night Pine (`#112f27`)**: Structural backdrops for hero displays, inverted editorial sections, and global footer anchors.
- **Electric Accent Lime (`#d7f36a`)**: The secondary beacon color, applied to hero kickers, primary conversion buttons over dark containers, and focal compass indicators.
- **Sunset Terracotta (`#e78d5b`)**: The tertiary directional accent, used for dynamic compass needles, departure callouts, and urgency badges.
- **Warm Paper Canvas (`#f7f8f4`)**: The global base canvas that eliminates digital glare and introduces tactile warmth.
- **Surfaces & Borders**: Pure white (`#ffffff`) is strictly elevated above paper for cards and search panels, framed by subtle natural hairlines (`#e4e9e2`).
- **Deep Ink (`#13251f`)**: High-contrast, near-black neutral for all primary copy and brand wordmarks.

## Typography

The typographic engine uses **Inter** calibrated with tight, print-editorial tracking rules:

- **Negative Tracking on Scale**: Any heading scaled above 24px aggressively tightens letter-spacing (from `-0.8px` down to `-3.5px`) with near-unity line height (`0.99` to `1.15`). This compresses word clusters into solid visual anchors that command attention without shouting.
- **Positive Tracking on Microcopy**: Labels and badges at 11px and smaller systematically apply all-caps letter-spacing (`+1.0px` to `+1.8px`) with ultra-bold weights (`800`–`850`), guaranteeing instant scannability on low-luminance containers.
- **Fluid Reading Rhythm**: Body paragraphs maintain expansive line-height ratios (`1.6` to `1.7`) on compact sizes (13px–15px) to support comfortable sustained reading of itinerary narratives.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a responsive fluid grid bound to a centered maximum content container of `1200px` (`min(1200px, calc(100% - 44px))` on desktop; `calc(100% - 28px)` on mobile).

- **Grid Rhythms**:
  - **Desktop (>900px)**: 12-column grid or flexible 4-column item grids with `16px` to `20px` gutters.
  - **Tablet (601px–900px)**: 2-column stacked modules with `16px` gutters.
  - **Mobile (≤600px)**: 1-column linear flow with `12px` gutters.
- **Spatial Pacing**:
  - Major section padding is generous: `4.25rem` (`68px`) on desktop and `3rem` (`48px`) on mobile.
  - Internal card padding follows a strict `17px` (`~1.06rem`) standard.
- **Negative Margin Overlaps**:
  - Search engines and floating control bars employ negative vertical margins (`-42px`) overlapping the lower hero boundary, visually locking header photography with booking utilities.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on ambient environmental shadows, subtle glass layers, and intentional perspective shifts rather than aggressive drop shadows.

- **Atmospheric Ambient Shadow**: A single universal depth token `0 18px 50px rgba(19, 37, 31, 0.09)` provides deep, weightless elevation for the search engine card, hover states on package cards, mobile flyout menus, and modals.
- **Tinted Container Shadow**: Preview cards on light surfaces employ soft sage tinting `0 14px 40px #36573a13`.
- **Glassmorphism & Frosted Plates**: Floating status indicators and in-card category pills use `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over semi-transparent white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` for light cards; `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)` for image overlays) framed by translucent strokes (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.46)`).
- **Tactile Scrapbook Perspective**: To break computational rigidity, preview itinerary cards use 3D perspective (`perspective: 1800px; transform: rotate(1deg)`) and brand mark shields use counter-rotation (`transform: rotate(-7deg)`).
- **Z-Index Hierarchy**:
  - `z-index: 1`: Card image overlays and pill tags.
  - `z-index: 2`: Hero floating search engine.
  - `z-index: 10`: Mobile navigation header and dropdowns.
  - `z-index: 20`: Toast notification banners.
  - `z-index: 30`: Global modal backdrops (`#0b211bd1`) and modal dialog containers.

## Shapes

The shape architecture operates on a deliberate dual system: full pill curvature for interactive controls and micro-badges, balanced by soft, structured rectangles for content surfaces.

- **Pill Geometry (`999px`)**: All primary, secondary, and ghost buttons; destination filter tags; status badges; rating chips; and toast bars are fully rounded pills.
- **Circular Elements (`50%`)**: Circular directional arrow buttons (`34px`), close triggers, and live status dots.
- **Structured Cards & Containers**:
  - Hero and conversion banner frames: `24px` to `28px`.
  - Search panel and destination cards: `20px`.
  - Package itinerary cards: `19px`.
  - Category explorer cards: `16px`.
  - Form inputs, selects, and icon containers: `11px`.
- **Strokes & Dividers**: All borders maintain a consistent `1px solid #e4e9e2`, with soft internal timeline dividers rendered in `1px solid #edf0e9`.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Evergreen (`.btn-dark`)**: Background `#174f40`, text `#ffffff`, pill-radius (`999px`), padding `12px 22px`. Hover shifts background to `#133f34` with `transform: translateY(-2px)`.
- **Accent Electric Lime (`.btn-lime`)**: Background `#d7f36a`, text `#13251f`, pill-radius, padding `12px 22px`. Hover increases brightness by 5% with `transform: translateY(-2px)`.
- **Light Outlined (`.btn-light`)**: Background `#ffffff`, text `#13251f`, border `1px solid #e4e9e2`, pill-radius. Hover transitions border to `#b6c7b9` and surface to `#f7f8f4`.
- **Circular Action Trigger (`.dest-arrow`, `.close`)**: 34px × 34px circle, background `#ffffffdb`, displaying an up-right arrow glyph (`↗`) or close icon (`×`).

### Cards & Structured Travel Containers
- **Destination Cards**: Height 340px–420px, border-radius `20px`, full-bleed image with overlay gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(5,24,18,.02) 20%, rgba(5,24,18,.82) 100%)`). Content aligns to bottom with glass tags (`background: #ffffff12; backdrop-filter: blur(8px); border: 1px solid #ffffff75; color: #ffffff`). On card hover, image scales smoothly (`1.045`).
- **Package Itinerary Cards**: Surface `#ffffff`, border-radius `19px`, border `1px solid #e4e9e2`, padding `17px`. Features taxonomy indicator pills (e.g., 🛏️ Stays, ☕ Dining, 🚆 Transport), package title (`18px`), rating badge (`#7a6a2e`), and dual footer with bold price and soft sage CTA button (`#edf4e7`). Hover elevates with `0 18px 50px rgba(19,37,31,.09)`.
- **Category Explorer Tiles**: Surface `#ffffff`, border-radius `16px`, border `1px solid #e4e9e2`, padding `14px 16px`. Displays prominent taxonomy emoji, bold label, and micro inventory count.

### Form Inputs & Search Engine
- **Floating Search Engine**: Surface `#ffffff`, border-radius `20px`, padding `17px`, shadow `0 18px 50px rgba(19,37,31,.09)`. Grid layout accommodating Destination, Dates, Guests, and Trip Type fields.
- **Input Fields & Selects**: Surface `#f5f7f3`, border `1px solid #e4e9e2`, border-radius `11px`, padding `11px 14px`. Focus state suppresses default outlines and activates a 2px green focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #b8d89e`).
- **Input Labels**: Uppercase micro-typography (`10px`, weight `800`, letter-spacing `+1.0px`, color `#829087`), stacked strictly above inputs.

### Chips & Booking Status Tokens
- **Live / Confirmed Status**: Soft sage pill (`#edf4e7`), text `#174f40`, featuring a 7px pulsing emerald dot (`#58ad7a`).
- **Limited Departure**: Pill with terracotta border (`#e78d5b`) and matching warm text.
- **Member Deal**: Electric lime pill (`#d7f36a`) with deep ink text (`#13251f`).

### Feedback & Overlay Modals
- **Toast Notifications**: Surface `#143f33`, text `#ffffff`, pill-shaped, elevated at `z-index: 20` with `0 18px 50px rgba(19,37,31,.2)`.
- **Booking Drawer & Modal**: Modal dialog surface `#ffffff`, border-radius `23px`, padding `24px`, isolated by modal scrim `#0b211bd1` (`z-index: 30`).
- **Compass Loader**: Custom 44px × 44px vector compass with evergreen ring (`#174f40`), terracotta needle (`#e78d5b`), and lime pivot dot (`#d7f36a`) driven by custom cubic-bezier spin physics.
