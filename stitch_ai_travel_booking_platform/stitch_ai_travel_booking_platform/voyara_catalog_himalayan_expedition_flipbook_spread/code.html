Spiti Valley: The Fossil Ridge & Millennium Cloister

Carved by the glacial retreat of the ancient Tethys Sea, Spiti unfolds at 3,800 meters as a sanctuary of wind-sheared shale, ninth-century mud-plastered monasteries, and undisturbed wildlife migration ridges.

Stage I · Day 1-2 · Tabo & Dhankar 3,050m → 3,890m

Meditation chambers at the UNESCO 996 CE Tabo Monastery. Cliffside citadel hike to Dhankar Lake rim overlooking the confluence of the Spiti and Pin rivers.

Marine ammonite fossil walks led by a village elder. Dispatch slow-mail postcards from the highest post box on Earth; twilight prayer ritual at Komic Gonpa.

Ramsar wetland sanctuary survey. Glacial moon-reflections under Bortle Class-1 dark skies accompanied by low-impact insulated field yurts.
