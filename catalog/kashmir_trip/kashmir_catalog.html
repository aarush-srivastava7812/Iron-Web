SECTION 8: DAY-BY-DAY ITINERARY
CHRONOLOGICAL PLAN

Day-by-Day Schedule

A balanced, weather-conscious flow designed to maximize natural daylight, alpine visibility, and cultural relaxation.

DAY 1 · 15 NOV
Delhi Arrival & Ascent to Gulmarg
08:50 AM arrival at Srinagar Airport. Meet chauffeur, scenic drive via Tangmarg to Snowline Lodge. Check-in, hot kahwa welcome, and evening meadow sunset walk.
DAY 2 · 16 NOV
Apharwat Peak Snow & Gondola Adventure
10:00 AM cable car ascent through Kongdoori to Apharwat Peak (Phase 2). Snow trek, hot mountain lunch at 3,000m, and evening bukhari fireside dinner.
DAY 3 · 17 NOV
Pine Forest Treks & Leisure in the Meadow
Guided pine forest nature walk, visit historic St. Mary’s Church and Maharaja Palace ruins. Evening sledging and leisure at Gulmarg golf course snow bowl.
DAY 4 · 18 NOV
Descent to Srinagar & Dal Lake Shikara
Check-out and transfer to Srinagar Slow Living Homestay. Afternoon sunrise shikara cruise, floating bazaar tour, and evening tea at Kashmir Corner Cafe.
DAY 5 · 19 NOV
Betaab Valley & Lidder River Day Excursion
Full-day private excursion to Pahalgam. Stroll through Betaab Valley, pine riverbanks, and walnut orchards. Evening return for 7-course royal Wazwan banquet.
DAY 6 · 20 NOV
Old Srinagar Heritage Walk & Souvenir Guild
Explore Jama Masjid, Shah-e-Hamdan, saffron spice bazaar, and master artisan workshops for authentic GI-tagged Pashmina shawls and papier-mâché crafts.
DAY 7 · 21 NOV
Farewell Noon Chai & Departure Flight
Relaxed morning breakfast with fresh bakery breads. Private transfer to Srinagar Airport for 03:40 PM departure flight back to New Delhi.
Winter Health & Altitude Care

Stay hydrated with warm water and saffron kahwa. Gulmarg sits at 2,650m and Apharwat at 3,980m; take gentle steps and carry lip balm, SPF 50 sunscreen, and moisturizing cream.

Authentic Shopping Guarantee

Look for Kashmir Government GI (Geographical Indication) tags on saffron and Pashmina. The guide will accompany you only to certified government artisan emporiums.
