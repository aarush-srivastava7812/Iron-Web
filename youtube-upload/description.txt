What happens when a traveler changes the budget after AI agents have already made a plan?

Iron Web is a shared agent-memory system demonstrated through Voyara, a travel-planning website. FalkorDB connects versioned requirements, their source evidence, and the work that depends on them.

This 2-minute 58-second demo shows a Kerala trip for two travelers, December 1–3, 2026. Seven hosted specialists create a draft using an INR 200,000 budget. The budget then changes to INR 120,000: the older plan becomes stale, and a new run captures the revised requirement and supporting evidence.

The video retains the supplied ElevenLabs narration and includes English captions burned into the picture.
