Hardware Profile & GPU Policy (Local Testing):
- Target GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop GPU (6GB VRAM total)
- Mandatory VRAM Buffer Rule: Leave at least 1.0GB VRAM buffer untouched at all times for OS, display, and desktop tasks.
- Operational Ceiling: Maximum aggregate VRAM usage for memory models and pipelines <= 5.0GB.
- Acceleration Targets:
  * Embeddings: BAAI/bge-small-en-v1.5 with INT8 quantization on CUDAExecutionProvider (~200MB VRAM).
  * Reranker: BAAI/bge-reranker-base cross-encoder on CUDAExecutionProvider (~1.2GB VRAM).
  * Ingestion/Batching: Conservative batch sizes (32 for embeddings, 16 for reranking) to prevent transient VRAM spikes.
  * Robustness: Automatic fallback to CPUExecutionProvider if CUDA memory limits or provider errors occur.
